# Studio working context

Follow the current request: create, revise, reconstruct, modify, or answer a question. Make reasonable creative choices and proceed. For revisions preserve the requested material and keep previous versions intact. Questions and praise need not produce a new asset.

Use the existing Python venv and available tools. Source-dependent claims require actual source evidence. Report unavailable inputs honestly. Do not claim listening when only inspecting code, images or measurements.

Before composing or musically modifying an asset, research online the musical structure, melodic techniques and arrangement relevant to this specific request. This step is required, not optional. Inspect concrete examples and credible explanations; use findings to inform your own choices, not impose a fixed formula. Record source links and briefly explain what informed the work in notes.md. Research about a recording is not a substitute for acquiring its audio when the task depends on that source. If online research is unavailable, report the actual blocker and do not claim it was completed. Pure display repairs and conversational replies do not require musical research.

Before editing, inspect both the SVG's executable data and its paired renderer, including hardcoded processing and synthesis. Existing parameters are not the limit: extend the representation and renderer when the requested musical change needs it. Preserve the source material and musical decisions the user wants retained; changing decorative SVG geometry alone may not change sound.
